# UAT Plan

Status: PLAN READY / EXECUTION PENDING DEP-008.

UAT must use named, approved testers and a production-like environment. Each scenario records tester, role, build/commit, input fixture/source, expected result, actual result, evidence link, defect, retest and acceptance.

- Sales: Customer, Contact, Project, Opportunity, Hot Lead/Won/Lost, Draft/Submitted/Under Negotiation/SENT/Supersede, activities and search.
- Management: team scope, View As, approvals, Company Target and analytics.
- Sales Support: support projections, import/export, DQ and authorized complaints without forbidden edits.
- Logistics: planning and shipment milestones through arrival; no Dispatch/Delivery/POD.
- Operations: capacity estimates at 900/700/600 and 24h/day, estimate only.
- Finance: FX, Payments, Cheques and Returned Scrap 25% approval.
- R&D: requested sizes and complaint insights, read-only negative tests.
- Client Portal: own orders/shipments/complaints and cross-client denial.
- Analytics: conversion, segments, top customers/countries, WON target, Local/Global, ERP separation and DATA_CONFLICT exclusion.
- UX: Arabic/RTL, English/LTR, desktop and mobile.

RG-11 requires executed evidence, defect disposition and Business acceptance; none is claimed.
